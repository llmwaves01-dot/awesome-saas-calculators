Dated observations from vendor pricing pages and help docs, recorded the day each page was read. Prices and plan names change quietly, so a figure without a date on it is a guess.

## What changed recently

- **Salesforce**, 3 September 2026: Enterprise and Unlimited were replaced by Core at $195, Advanced at $395 and Max at $550 per user per month.
- **Close**, September 2026: all four plans renamed. Base to Solo, Startup to Essentials, Professional to Growth, Enterprise to Scale.
- **monday CRM**, September 2026: the 6,000 AI credits advertised on lower plans are a one-time grant in monday's own plan-types doc, not a monthly allowance.
- **Freshsales**, September 2026: included incoming call minutes are 2,000, 3,000 and 5,000 per account by plan, with no rollover.
- **Outreach**, September 2026: no dollar figures published at all. Every tier is quote-only.

## Why the sticker price is the smallest part

Seat minimums, onboarding fees, credit grants that expire and per-minute calling add-ons all land after signature.
