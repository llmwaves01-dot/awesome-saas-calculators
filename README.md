
# Awesome SaaS Calculators

A curated list of free calculators for SaaS buyers, founders and operators.

Every tool on this list is free to use with **no sign-up and no email gate**.
If a tool starts gating results behind a form, open an issue and it comes off
the list.

## Contents

- [For buyers](#for-buyers)
- [For founders and operators](#for-founders-and-operators)
- [Contributing](#contributing)

## For buyers

Working out whether a subscription is worth paying for.

- [SaaS ROI Calculator](https://www.saasswitcher.com/tools/saas-roi-calculator) - Turns seats, cost per seat and hours saved into ROI %, net annual value, payback period and total hours saved per year. Saves to your browser, nothing sent to a server.

## For founders and operators

Measuring the health of a SaaS business.

- [Founderpath Free Tools](https://founderpath.com/free-tools) - 35 calculators covering ARR, MRR, burn rate, churn, NRR, cash runway, quick ratio, CAC, LTV and payback period. No signup or email required.
- [Saasly](https://saasly.app/) - 24+ metrics including MRR, ARR, ARPU, gross margin, CAC, ROAS and churn, plus a benchmark library and glossary. Runs locally in the browser, no data leaves your device.
- [daydream SaaS Calculators](https://www.withdaydream.com/tools/saas-calculators) - CAC, NRR, churn, CLV, LTV:CAC, MRR and ARR, each benchmarked against real B2B companies between $2M and $50M ARR.
- [LogicLibrary SaaS Calculators](https://logiclibrary.net/saas/) - LTV, CAC, MRR, NRR, burn and runway calculators.

## Contributing

Pull requests welcome. To add a calculator:

1. Fork this repository
2. Add your entry to the relevant section, keeping the existing format:
   `- [Name](url) - One-line description of what it calculates.`
3. Open a pull request

Please only submit tools that are genuinely free and do not require a
sign-up or email address to see results.
